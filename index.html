Safety Shield
Human verification is a required handover step.
Cura deliberately does not convert OCR text into an active medication list. A transcription can contain omissions, substitutions, or character errors—especially with handwriting.

This prototype does not provide clinical decision support. No medicine, strength, route, frequency, or duration should be acted upon based solely on extracted text.


Handover checklist
□ Original prescription is available and legible.
□ Extracted text has been compared field-by-field with the source.
□ Unclear or conflicting entries have been escalated.
□ Verification is documented by an authorized healthcare professional in the appropriate clinical system.






Project overview
Cura — Medication Continuity & Safe Handover
Problem statement: “The Forgotten Prescription.” Cura is a prototype workflow for preserving prescription documents and their transcription during care transitions.
What this demo includes
  • Browser-based image upload
  • Backend OCR integration (Gemini API when configured)
  • SQLite scan metadata and transcription history
  • Explicit unverified status and safety messaging
What it does not do
  • Diagnose, prescribe, or recommend treatment
  • Automatically correct handwriting or infer missing values
  • Replace a pharmacist, clinician, or electronic health record
